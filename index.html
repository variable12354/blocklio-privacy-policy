Privacy Policy

Summary: Blocklio is a casual puzzle game designed with player privacy in mind. We do not require accounts, we do not collect personal identifiers (such as your name, email, or contacts), and all game saves, coins, and booster balances are stored locally on your device. Standard anonymous diagnostics and advertising SDKs (Google AdMob and Google Firebase) are utilized to operate the game.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your personal privacy:

  • No Accounts or Registration: You can download and enjoy Blocklio immediately without creating an account or logging in.
  • No Direct Personal Information: We do not ask for, harvest, or store your name, physical address, email, phone number, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.
  • No Financial or Credit Card Data: All In-App Purchases (e.g. coin bundles, theme passes, ad removal) are securely processed through Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Blocklio stores gameplay progress directly on your device using secure local storage (AsyncStorage). This data stays on your device:

  • Progress & Records: Levels completed, high scores, adventure mode stars, and daily challenge milestones.
  • Virtual Inventory: In-game coin balance, booster inventories (Undo, Hammer, Shuffle, Wild Piece), and unlocked themes.
  • Player Preferences: Sound effect volume, music toggle, haptic vibration preferences, and active block theme.

Notice: Uninstalling the application or wiping app storage in device settings may permanently reset your local game save and coin balance.

3. Third-Party Services & Automated Telemetry

To keep Blocklio free, reliable, and properly balanced, we integrate standard, verified SDKs from Google:

3.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner ads, interstitial transitions between levels, and voluntary rewarded video ads (e.g. watching an ad to earn extra coins or revive a game).

  • Data Processed: Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud protection), device model, carrier, screen dimensions, and ad interaction metrics.
  • Purpose: Serving ads, preventing fraudulent impressions, and providing opt-in rewards.
  • Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Google Privacy Policy • Google Advertising Technologies

3.2 Google Analytics for Firebase

Used to monitor anonymous game events (such as levels completed, combo streaks, and session lengths) to improve difficulty balance and fix progression bottlenecks.

  • Data Processed: Anonymous gameplay events, app instance IDs, device model, OS version, country code, and session duration.
  • Firebase Privacy & Security

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics

Used to immediately detect and diagnose crashes so we can release fast stability updates.

  • Data Processed: Crash stack traces, memory usage at time of crash, device model, and OS version. No personally identifiable data is included.
  • Firebase Crashlytics Terms

3.4 Firebase Remote Config & Play Integrity

Used for remote game configuration, checking for important game updates, and validating software integrity to protect against cheating.

4. Android Device Permissions

Blocklio requests only minimal permissions strictly needed for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for ads, crash reports, remote configuration, and analytics.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Triggers haptic feedback vibration when placing blocks and making line clears (can be toggled off inside game Settings).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Blocklio is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not contain chat rooms, public profiles, or social media integration. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

EEA / UK Users (GDPR): We process data based on your consent (for personalized advertisements where applicable) and legitimate interest (app security, crash diagnostics, and anti-fraud). You have the right to request access, rectification, or deletion of data, which can also be accomplished by clearing app storage or deleting your Advertising ID.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information for financial payment. Sharing pseudonymous advertising IDs with advertising partners may be classified as "sharing" under California law. You can opt out of interest-based ads via your device's Google Ads settings.

7. Data Retention & Security

All communication with Google Firebase and AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Anonymous telemetry is retained in accordance with standard Google Cloud retention schedules (2 to 14 months) before automatic deletion.
